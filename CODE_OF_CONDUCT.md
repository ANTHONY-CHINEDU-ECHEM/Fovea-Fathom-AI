# Code of conduct

This project brings together clinicians, engineers and researchers. We expect everyone to:

* treat others with respect and assume good intent;
* give and accept constructive criticism, especially about clinical safety, without personal attack;
* protect patients: never share identifiable information in issues, pull requests or discussions;
* welcome people of every background, experience level and profession.

Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact listed in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers will review reports promptly and confidentially and may warn, suspend or remove contributors who breach this code.
